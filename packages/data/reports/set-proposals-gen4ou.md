# Gen 4 OU — proposed set changes

These come after the build fixes:

- **Whole-set move picking.**
- **Choice sets skip useless moves.** They drop status moves (but keep Trick, Healing Wish and Memento) and Focus Punch, Flail and Reversal.
- **Natures fit the EV spread.**

Note that Gen 4 OU bans sleep moves. That's why Venusaur, Exeggutor and Breloom don't get Sleep Powder or Spore; those flags are fine.

## Keep: flags that are actually fine

- **Hand-added but real sets:** Azelf Nasty Plot and Choice Specs, Roserade Toxic Spikes lead and Choice Specs, Weavile Swords Dance, Porygon-Z Specs and Scarf, Gallade Swords Dance and Scarf, Electivire Choice Band, Vaporeon Wish, Shaymin SubSeed and Specs, Honchkrow Nasty Plot, Crobat Choice Band, Raikou Specs, Jolteon Substitute + Baton Pass, Tentacruel offensive spinner.
- **"No STAB" that's right for the set:** Clefable, Forretress, Registeel, Porygon2, Weezing, Cloyster, Azelf lead, Blissey.
- **Reasonable alternate picks:** Latias, Starmie, Heatran, Flygon, Dragonite Choice Band, the Rotom formes, Donphan, Breloom, Celebi, Hippowdon, Venusaur, Mesprit, Drapion, Kangaskhan, Steelix, Spiritomb.
- **EVs and natures:** Modest Empoleon with Aqua Jet, Bold Claydol with Earthquake, Jolly Exeggutor screens with Explosion. All are deliberate.
