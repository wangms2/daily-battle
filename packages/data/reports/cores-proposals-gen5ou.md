# Gen 5 OU: cores, round 3

Every core has **a vetted source** (a Smogon article, the ADV guide, or a thread post other players reacted to) and passes **checks on default sets**.

- **Defensive cores must not share a weakness.** If two members take 2× or more from the same type, something in the core has to resist it (a trio can cover it with its third member). **Offensive cores may share weaknesses.** The checks column still shows them.
- **Reactions** are the likes on the post that presents the core, from the thread pages (approximate; counts come as "named + N others"). A post listing many cores shares its count, so it vouches for the post, not each core.
- **Weaknesses covered:** how many of the members' weak spots another member resists or is immune to. Abilities count (Levitate, Volt Absorb, …), and a Mega counts as its Mega forme.
- **Top-15:** members among the 15 most-used species; pools allow at most four.
- No core contains another as a subset, and every one passes the validator together on default sets.
- Species in 3 or more cores: Scizor (3).
